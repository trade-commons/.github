# Code of Conduct

The Global Trade Commons adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in full. The canonical text is linked rather than reproduced here, so that this file cannot drift from
it.

In short: participation is open to everyone regardless of background or affiliation, and behaviour
that makes the space unwelcoming is not tolerated. Disagreement about technical direction is expected
and welcome; disparagement of the people holding a position is not.

Two points matter particularly in a standards context. Participants frequently represent competing
commercial interests, and the work only holds together if a contribution is judged on its merits
rather than on who submitted it. Participants also represent governments and institutions that
disagree with each other, and this organisation is not where those disagreements are conducted.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the organisation maintainers. Until the foundation is constituted and has named a
contact, use a private report through GitHub or the address published on
<https://trade-commons.github.io>. Reports are handled confidentially, and a reporter is never
required to raise a matter with the person concerned first.
